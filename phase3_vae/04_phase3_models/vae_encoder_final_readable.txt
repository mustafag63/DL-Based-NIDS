--- VAE-specific notes (not captured in this model's own Keras config) ---
latent_dim = 10
beta (KL weight, selected variant from section 9.3) = 0.25
z_mean layer name: 'z_mean' (Dense, linear activation, no clipping)
z_log_var layer name: 'z_log_var' -> clipped to [-10, 10] via a Lambda layer (numerical stability - see section 2, prevents exp() overflow) before being used downstream.
Reparameterization trick (applied in VAE2.call(), NOT part of this saved encoder model - the encoder only outputs z_mean/z_log_var):
  eps = tf.random.normal(shape=tf.shape(z_mean))
  z   = z_mean + tf.exp(0.5 * z_log_var) * eps
z is then fed into the decoder (this repo's vae_decoder_final.keras) to produce the reconstruction.
